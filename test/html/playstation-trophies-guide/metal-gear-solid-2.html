• Love Hurts

    Watch Rose kill Raiden on a rooftop

    After clearing the game and making a clear save (your save data will be red). From the main menu go to the following options; Special > Casting Theater. Press once to select "Demo 8 (Solidus VS Raiden: The Final Battle)." Now change Raiden's character to Rose and Solidus's character to Raiden and play the clip. Watch the entire clip through to earn a quick trophy.

  • Steel Grip

    Attain grip level 3

    This can be done as Snake or Raiden and in numerous places throughout the game, mainly wherever is a ledge you can dangle from. While dangling from a ledge press and hold + to have Snake/Raiden perform a pull-up. You will have to do a total of 200 pull-ups to reach grip level 3. You know you reached the next grip level as grip level 2 is yellow and grip level 3 is grey. A successful pull-up is from a dead-hang and holding it until Snake/Raiden's head stops shaking as he pulls himself up. If you release the buttons before then, the pull-up may not be counter. You should also do this with some sort of ground beneath your feet so you don't fall to your death. There's a faster way to level your grip gauge. Hang over a ledge with a ledge below you; press to drop and then quickly press to grab onto the ledge below your original ledge. A ledge catch counts as 10 pullups.

    Note: Snake must reach grip level 2 before reaching the holds in the Tanker to get every dog tag.

  • Great Dane

    Collect all dog tags

    The trophy description here is misleading. It says to collect all dog tags, but all you really need to do is collect all unlockable items. There are 6 items in total; 2 of which are in the Tanker scenario and will require almost 3 full dog tag collections to earn. The other 4 are in Plant scenario and will require collecting every dog tag in the Plant scenario. The final item in the Tanker scenario is the Stealth which is rewarded upon collecting 76 Tanker Dog tags; the final Plant scenario item is the Blue Wig which is rewarded upon collecting all (218) Plant Dog tags.

    To get a guard to relinquish his dog tag; you need to sneak up behind them and ready your weapon at them, Snake/Raiden will say "Freeze!" and the guard will put his hands up and freeze. Keeping pressed, hold and move to the front of the guard. Go into first person view and point your weapon at their head or groin, most guards will get scared and shake their dog tag loose. Some guards refuse to budge and need more coaxing; equip a lethal weapon and shoot near their body or in a limb and they'll lose their resolve. Point your weapon at their head or groin again and they'll surrender their dog tag.
